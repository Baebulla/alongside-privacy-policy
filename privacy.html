Privacy Policy for Alongside

Last updated: July 2026

Alongside ("the App") is an accountability and focus app developed by Baebulla Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect.

Information we do NOT collect

Alongside does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not require an account, login, or sign-up of any kind
  • Does not collect your name, email, location, or contact information
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services
  • Does not send your data to the developer or any third party
  • Has no backend server — there is nowhere for your data to go

Information stored on your device

The App saves your commitments, focus session history, streaks, companion progress, and settings locally on your own device only, using standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the App, or using its "Delete All Data" option, permanently removes this data.

Notifications

Alongside schedules reminder and summary notifications (for example, "it's time to start" or a nightly recap) entirely on your device, using your device's own notification system. These notifications are generated from your locally stored data and are never sent from, or reported back to, a server.

Internet permission

The App's installation package includes the standard Android internet permission used by the underlying app framework. Alongside does not use this permission to send, receive, or transmit any personal or usage data.

Children's privacy

Alongside does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if accounts, cloud sync, or online features are ever added), this page will be updated and the date above will reflect the change.
